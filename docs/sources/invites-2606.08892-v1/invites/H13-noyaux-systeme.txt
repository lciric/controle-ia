You are designing a diverse set of "idea kernels" for high-quality ML-experiment-proposal generators.  Each kernel is a 1-2 sentence description of a *stylistic and methodological angle* that a generator  might emphasize when proposing experiments — for instance, theory-leaning vs empirical, ablation-heavy,  mechanism-focused, scaling-laws-oriented, ICLR-flashy, conservative-and-rigorous, etc.
Your kernels must be diverse: cover different scientific styles, different priorities (rigor vs novelty
vs feasibility), different communication registers, different methodological commitments. They should
all be plausibly producible by a competent ML researcher — none should be sandbagging or low-effort.
