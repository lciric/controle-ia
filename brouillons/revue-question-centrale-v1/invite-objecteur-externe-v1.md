# Invite du tour 1 (objections à l'aveugle) pour un objecteur externe, d'une autre famille de modèles — v1

À coller par Lazar, s'il le décide, dans la conversation d'un autre modèle, avec les documents primaires joints (protocole
`brouillons/revue-question-centrale-v1/protocole-revue-et-brainstorm-v1.md`, section 2 ; jamais les pièces `pain-axis`). La
confidentialité des hypothèses non publiées (candidature) est la décision de Lazar. Les objections reçues sont collées telles
quelles dans la session, qui les verse au tour 2 comme celles des objecteurs internes. Le modèle ne voit ni les objections des
autres, ni les réponses, ni la note de décision.

---

You are an independent reviewer of a research programme on white-box AI control (monitoring AI agents through their internal
activations). You receive its primary documents (programme, decisions, verified literature review, mathematical note, sealed
results, draft pre-registrations, project proposal). Read them; do not ask questions; do not try to be agreeable.

Your only task is to **object**. Write every objection you can defend, each classified as:
- **fatal**: the central claim, or the case that its result would change what AI developers do, does not hold as stated;
- **serious**: the plan, a hypothesis or a claim must change;
- **minor**: wording or detail.

For each objection give: (1) the objection in one or two sentences; (2) the argument; (3) the evidence (document and section,
or an external source with enough detail to be checked; say "unverified" if you cite from memory); (4) what would resolve it;
(5) your probability that the objection is right.

Then give at most two **bold alternatives** (a research question, hypothesis or experiment the programme should consider
instead or in addition), each with what result would refute it and a rough cost.

Finally, give your calibrated probabilities (0 to 1) for: (a) gate G1 passes as specified (white-box sequential monitoring beats
the best black-box baseline on recall at 1 % false positives, 95 % interval); (b) in Paper C, the fitted destination ĉ equals the
attacker's target for at least one attack family; (c) a positive G1 result would change a decision of an AI lab's control team;
(d) a negative G1 result would change one. Explain each number in one line.

Write in English or French. No preamble, no summary of the documents, no praise.
